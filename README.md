# 💗 OUR LITTLE WORLD — Coco × Modak

An interactive, deeply romantic digital love experience created specifically for **Chanchal (Coco / Bayko)** by **Darshan (Modak)**.

## ✨ Features

1. **Magical Landing & Sound Consent**: "Hey Bayko... ❤️" with breathing pulsating heart and polite audio prompt.
2. **7 Cinematic Story Chapters**: From polytechnic Instagram message, to June 2025 blooming love, to our 15 Feb 2026 Navra-Bayko sacred beginning.
3. **Bouquet Garden**: Custom interactive flower arranger with roses, tulips, daisies, sunflowers, cherry blossoms, and lavender.
4. **Physical 3D Love Letter**: Interactive wax-sealed envelope that unfolds with handwritten love letters and secret P.S. reveal.
5. **Kiss Corner 💋**: Interactive kiss counter with progressive affectionate reactions (1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20, 30 kisses) and lipstick print tribute (03 • 09 • 2026).
6. **Compliment Universe & Infinite Reason Generator (∞)**: 20+ personalized reasons plus an infinite reason generator and the famous "Coco's Favorite Hobby: Eating Modak 😂" alert.
7. **Love Quiz**: "How Well Do You Know Your Modak?" featuring personalized couple questions and guaranteed 100% husband privileges.
8. **Memory Lane**: 10 milestone memories with photos, dates, tags, Ganpati Bappa blessing illustration, and the break-dance ride mela story.
9. **Soundtrack Room**: 10 curated songs + "I Wanna Be Yours" (Our Song) + "Khat" (Darshan's favorite for Coco) with a vinyl player and Web Audio ambient synthesizer fallback.
10. **Catch My Heart Mini Game**: Interactive falling heart game with sweet nicknames (Coco, Bayko, My Safe Place, Humsafar...).
11. **Surprise Box 🎁**: Glowing 3D gift box with multi-stage unboxing, lifetime supply of eternal love coupon, and "Accept Forever ❤️".
12. **The Climax ("One Last Thing…")**: Slow cinematic text reveals, heartbeat pulse, and the complete personal vows of eternal love.
13. **Secret Easter Eggs & Achievements**: Clickable crescent moon, secret Modak icon, 8 romantic collectible achievement badges, daily love notes generator.
14. **Full Persistence & PWA**: Saves progress in LocalStorage with replay option.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or run with any static server:

```bash
uv run python -m http.server 5173
```
Then visit `http://localhost:5173`.
